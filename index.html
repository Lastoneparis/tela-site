Tela

Notes, documents & databases — fast, private, offline-first.

Everything lives on your device, not on a server, so Tela opens instantly, works anywhere, and your data stays yours. No account required. Native on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro.

What Tela does

  • Instant search across notes, documents, databases, rows, and fields — accent-insensitive, so "cafe" finds "café".
  • Databases with typed fields and tappable URL links, plus a drag-and-drop Kanban board with colored columns.
  • A calm editor with headings, checklists, and code.
  • Today — everything due, gathered from your notes and databases, plus reminders you set yourself.
  • Tela Assist — on-device AI that summarizes, cleans up writing, and pulls out action items.

Free forever

Unlimited notes, instant search, Today, reminders, up to 3 databases, all views, and 3 Tela Assist runs a month. Premium unlocks unlimited on-device Assist, Markdown export, and unlimited databases.
