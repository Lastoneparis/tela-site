Tela Support

Tela is a fast, private, offline-first workspace for notes, documents, and databases. Everything lives on your device, so it opens instantly and works anywhere — no account required.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and start writing in the editor — headings, checklists, and code are all built in.
  2. Turn any list into a database with typed fields, tappable URL links, and a drag-and-drop Kanban board.
  3. Check Today for everything due, gathered from your notes and databases, plus reminders you set yourself.
  4. Use Tela Assist to summarize, clean up writing, or pull out action items — it runs entirely on your device.

Frequently asked

Is my data private? Yes — everything is stored locally on your device. Tela has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

Does Tela work offline? Yes. Tela is offline-first: open and edit anywhere, with no loading spinners and nothing to sign in to.

What's free? Unlimited notes, instant search, Today, reminders, up to 3 databases, all views, and 3 Tela Assist runs a month — free forever.

What does Premium add? Unlimited on-device Tela Assist (summaries, bullet points, cleanup, action items, title suggestions), Markdown export (documents and databases as Markdown tables), and unlimited databases. Premium is a Monthly or Annual subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase; prices are shown in-app.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription? Open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions, or manage it in your Apple Account settings.

How do I restore a purchase? In Tela, open Settings → Restore Purchases.
